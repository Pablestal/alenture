Read `CLAUDE.md`, then draft a milestone brief for: $ARGUMENTS

Do not write any code. Produce the brief only, in this format:

## Context for the change
Why this is being built now, and what decision it rests on. Skip if obvious.

## Definition of done
One paragraph, written as something to *do* rather than something to read.
"I click a marker and see its details" — not "the detail panel is implemented."

## Scope
Numbered sections. Each names files or folders. Where a decision is genuinely
open, say so and ask rather than picking silently — mark those clearly.

## Out of scope
What's deliberately excluded, and why. This section is not optional: it's what
stops a milestone from growing while it's being built.

## Notes
Anything learned in an earlier milestone that bears on this one.

Then stop and wait. The brief gets reviewed before anything is built.
